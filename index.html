HOW IT WORKS — RULES & ARCHITECTURE

Understanding the strategic game design and AI pipeline.

1. The Dual-Dice Decision Problem

In traditional Snake & Ladder, the dice roll dictates every move. Here, every turn rolls two dice and the active player must select one value. Overshooting cell 100 is strictly illegal (if both dice overshoot, your turn is skipped).

2. Shield Deflection Strategy

Each player holds 2 shields. When you land on a snake head, you may spend a shield to deflect the snake and stay on that cell. The AI only uses a shield if the calculated position loss exceeds a dynamic reserve threshold, which halves in the endgame (cells ≥ 75).

3. The AI Decision Engine Formula

Final_Score = 0.60 × A*_Score + 0.40 × Logistic_Win_Probability

A* evaluates the safest path along the board graph, while Logistic Regression predicts long-term game win probabilities trained on 10,000 simulated games.
