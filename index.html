📖 Game Rules & Strategy Guide

How AI-Powered Snake & Ladder transforms chance into deep strategic gameplay.

1. The Dual Dice Mechanic

In traditional Snake & Ladder, the dice make all decisions for you. In this game, each player rolls two dice on their turn and chooses one die value to play. You must weigh the short-term distance gain against potential snake hazards ahead or ladder opportunities. An exact roll is required to finish at cell 100 (overshoots are prohibited).

2. Shield Energy Reserve

Each player starts with 2 shields. When your piece lands on a snake head, you may spend a shield to deflect the snake and remain safely on that cell. Because shields are scarce, the AI evaluates whether the positional benefit of preserving the head exceeds a dynamic reserve threshold. In the endgame (cells ≥ 75), shields are far more valuable!

3. The AI Decision Formula

The AI evaluates legal candidate moves using a weighted synthesis of search and machine learning:

Final_Score = 0.60 × A*_Score + 0.40 × Logistic_Win_Probability

A* ensures path safety and rapid progression toward the goal, while Logistic Regression estimates the long-term win probability derived from 10,000 simulated games.
